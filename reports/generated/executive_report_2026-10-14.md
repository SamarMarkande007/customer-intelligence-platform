# Executive Customer Report
*As of 2026-06-30 · generated 2026-10-14*

## Executive summary  
We have 8,000 customers, of which 6,009 are active, yielding a churn rate of 24.9% and a latest monthly churn of 4.0% (June 2026). Monthly recurring revenue stands at $81,365 with an ARPU of $13.54, but we are losing roughly $18,821 each month to churn. The 1,149 high‑risk customers identified by our churn model represent $14,225 of monthly revenue at risk, yet the model is modest (ROC‑AUC ≈ 0.62) and only covers churn dates from October 2025 onward. The biggest pain points are cost, competition, and content gaps, and churn is especially high in South Korea (29.3%), Germany (27.9%) and Indonesia (26.9%).  

## Recommended actions  
1. **Target high‑risk segment** – Deploy a retention offer to the 1,149 high‑risk customers, aiming to protect the $14,225/month at risk and directly curb the 4.0% monthly churn.  
2. **Address top churn reasons** – Roll out a pricing‑flexibility pilot and a content‑expansion roadmap to mitigate the “Too expensive” (365 complaints) and “Not enough content” (217 complaints) drivers.  
3. **Improve support experience** – Prioritize fixes for Billing & Payments (236 complaints, +53%) and Streaming Quality (201 complaints, +83%) to lift the CSAT from 3.26/5 and reduce the 6.6% failed‑payment rate.  
4. **Localize for high‑churn markets** – Launch localized pricing and feature bundles in South Korea, Germany, and Indonesia to bring their churn rates (29.3%, 27.9%, 26.9%) closer to the global average.
